Conference Scope

AIQIA 2027 brings together engineering, technology, management, finance, marketing, law and society to explore responsible, inclusive and impactful innovation in artificial intelligence, quantum technologies and emerging digital solutions.

Special Issues and Proposals

All accepted and presented papers of AIQIA 2027 will be published in the conference proceedings. Publication details will be announced soon.

Special proposals for workshops and invited sessions are also welcome.
