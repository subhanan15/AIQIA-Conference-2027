Checklist

  1. Address all reviewer comments in the final manuscript.
  2. Format the paper using the conference paper template; do not add page numbers.
  3. Validate the final PDF as instructed in the acceptance email.
  4. Complete the copyright form shared with accepted authors.
  5. Register at least one author before the camera-ready deadline.
  6. Upload the final PDF to Microsoft CMT.
