International participants may require a visa to enter India. Most nationalities can apply for an e-Conference Visa or e-Visa via the official portal of the Government of India.

  • Official portal: indianvisaonline.gov.in
  • Apply well in advance — processing times vary by nationality.
  • A conference invitation letter and political/event clearance may be required for conference visas.
