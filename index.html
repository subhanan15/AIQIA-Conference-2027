About the conference

About AIQIA 2027

AIQIA 2027 focuses on industrial and emerging frontier technologies, applications of electronics, controls, communications, instrumentation and computational intelligence — with a special emphasis on artificial intelligence and quantum technologies, their innovations and real-world applications.

The objective of the conference is to provide high-quality research and professional interactions for the advancement of science, technology and fellowship. Papers with new research results are encouraged for submission.

About SIT Hyderabad

Symbiosis Institute of Technology (SIT) Hyderabad, established in 2024, is one of the newest constituent institutes of Symbiosis International University (SIU), Pune — a recognised leader in India's private university sector.

Inspired by the vision of Chancellor Dr S.B. Mujumdar and founded on the guiding philosophy of "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" (the world is one family), SIT Hyderabad is committed to delivering world-class technical education in a vibrant and inclusive academic environment.

Located in Hyderabad, a city ranked among the best in India for livability, innovation, education and technology-driven growth, the institute is rapidly emerging as a hub for high-quality engineering education.
